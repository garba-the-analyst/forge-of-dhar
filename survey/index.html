Shaping Dhar

A survey for lecturers, researchers, students and developers.

Dhar is a new systems programming language and toolchain, built independently in Nigeria. It aims for the speed of C, the safety of Java and the readability of Python, with no LLVM or JVM underneath. The goal is a language people can learn, teach, research and build real things in.

It takes 8 to 12 minutes, depending on which parts apply to you. Your answers decide what gets built and how it is taught.

Your answers are emailed to Abdullahi Baba Garba, who leads the project. They are sent from your own email app, so he will see the address you send from, and nothing leaves your device until you press Send there. A PDF copy of your questions and answers is made and saved on your device, and you can attach it to the email. Answers are never published with your name. This page collects nothing itself and loads nothing from other sites.
